Your goal

What are you protecting your time for? Write a goal for today ("Finish the Q3 report, reply to Sam") or one for all time ("Build things, don't scroll"). It's shown every time a site is blocked and on the day review. You can change it any time, even during a block window, but it can't be empty, and other settings can't be saved until it's set.

Sites

Each site covers its subdomains. Full block redirects to the block page. No block only applies grayscale (if on) and tracks time. Tightening changes (enabling, full block, grayscale on) always work; loosening ones need an unlock during a block window.

Global schedule

Used by every site set to “global schedule”. Times are in your current timezone.
